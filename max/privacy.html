Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026 · Universal Insurance Brokers (“UIB”, “we”, “us”)

What MAX does

MAX is a quote-request app offered by Universal Insurance Brokers, a licensed Florida insurance agency. It collects the details an agent needs to prepare a car insurance quote and sends them to our office.

Information we collect

Only what you enter or photograph in the app: your name, phone number, email address, a photo of your driver's license and the details on it (name, date of birth, license number, address), a photo of your vehicle identification number (VIN) and the vehicle it identifies, and your current insurance company.

How we use it

To prepare and deliver your car insurance quote, to contact you about that quote by phone, text or email, and to place a policy with an insurance company if you choose to buy one. We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Your request is emailed to our quotes inbox and stored in our quoting system, where licensed UIB agents work on it. Photos are read by an automated document-reading service (Anthropic's Claude) solely to transcribe the fields, and are not used to train that service. When you ask us to quote, your details are shared with the insurance companies we quote with, as required to produce a rate.

Storage and retention

Requests are kept as long as needed to serve you and as Florida insurance record-keeping rules require. You may ask us to delete your request at any time by emailing quotes@universalinsurancebroker.com.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera only when you tap a photo button. Photos are resized on your device and sent securely over HTTPS. The app does not read your photo library in the background.

Children

MAX is intended for licensed drivers aged 18 and over.
