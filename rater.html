Add to your phone: install the Auto Rater as an app for one-tap access.
How rating works: each carrier is rated one of three ways. API – the app sends the quote to your secure rating function, which signs in with the credentials you store in Supabase (never in the browser) and returns the premium. Portal – the app opens the carrier's quoting site and copies a one-page quote summary so you can paste it. Manual – you type the premium you got from the carrier and the app keeps it in the comparison. Full setup steps are in RATER-SETUP.md.
